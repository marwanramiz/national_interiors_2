Premium UPVC Doors & Windows

With strong UPVC profiles and stylish glass options, they provide a perfect combination of security, comfort, natural light, and low maintenance for residential and commercial buildings.

02

Toughened Glass & Stainless Steel Handrail

Premium Toughened Glass Handrail combined with durable Stainless Steel fittings, designed to provide safety without compromising on style. Ideal for staircases, balconies, terraces, and modern commercial spaces.

Curved Toughened Glass Handrail

Elegant Curved Toughened Glass Handrails designed to enhance the beauty and safety of modern staircases. The curved stainless steel handrail follows the staircase profile, while the clear decorative glass panels create a stylish and open appearance. Ideal for residential and commercial interiors.

Toughened Glass Design Door

Stylish Toughened Glass Doors featuring elegant decorative patterns, premium fittings, and a modern frameless appearance. Designed to bring a combination of natural light, spaciousness, durability, and sophisticated aesthetics to homes, offices, and commercial interiors.

Toughened Glass Canopy

Elegant and durable Toughened Glass Canopies designed to provide reliable weather protection while enhancing the exterior appearance of homes and commercial buildings. The frameless glass design creates a modern, spacious look with excellent natural light transmission. Suitable for entrances, balconies, porches, and outdoor areas.

Toughened Glass Roof

Premium Toughened Glass Roofing designed to provide excellent weather protection while maintaining a bright and open environment. Our Story

About Us

From a Small Shop to National Interiors

Every successful journey has a beginning. For National Interiors, that beginning was a small shop, a simple dream and the courage to keep moving forward even during the hardest days.

Our journey began in 2016, when I started a small shop under the name NATIONAL TRADERS, with the valuable financial support and encouragement of my uncle, Mr. Abdullah.

At that time, we did not have a large investment, a big showroom or a well-established business. We started with very limited resources. What we had was the willingness to work hard, learn from every opportunity and build something of our own.

Our First Order

One of the most memorable moments in our journey was receiving our very first order — a 4 × 4 Aluminium Window for a site at Omampuliyur.

It may have been a small order in terms of size, but for us, it meant much more than that. It was the first step towards the future of National Interiors.

That first project gave us confidence. From there, we slowly started taking up more work, learning new skills and understanding the needs of our customers. One project led to another, and little by little, the small business began to grow.

The Difficult Days

However, our journey was never easy.

There were many difficult days during the early stages of the business. My friend Balachandar and I worked together through some extremely challenging financial situations. There were times when we struggled even to arrange a proper meal, but we continued working because we believed that our hard work would eventually create a better future.

Those difficult days taught us lessons that no business school could teach us. They taught us to value every customer, respect every opportunity, manage every rupee carefully and, most importantly, never give up when things become difficult.

Growing Step by Step

We did not grow overnight.

We grew one customer at a time, one project at a time and one lesson at a time.

Starting from Aluminium works, we gradually expanded our knowledge and services. With the support of our family, friends, customers and well-wishers, NATIONAL TRADERS continued to develop.

As our experience and capabilities grew, our vision also grew.

Today, that small beginning has developed into NATIONAL INTERIORS, providing a wide range of solutions including:

  • UPVC Doors & Windows
  • Modular Kitchens
  • Wardrobes
  • TV Units
  • Aluminium Interior Works
  • Toughened Glass Works
  • Stainless Steel Handrails & Gates
  • Complete Interior Solutions

We Remember Where We Started

Today, when we look back at our journey, we don't forget the small shop where everything began.

We don't forget Mr. Abdullah, whose financial support helped us take our first step.

We don't forget Balachandar, who stood alongside us during some of our most difficult days.

We don't forget our first 4 × 4 Aluminium Window at Omampuliyur.

And most importantly, we don't forget the customers who trusted us when we were still a small and developing business.

Every one of these memories is part of the foundation on which National Interiors stands today.

Our Promise

Our past has taught us that trust cannot be bought — it has to be earned.

That is why we continue to focus on quality workmanship, reliable service, honest communication and customer satisfaction.

For us, every project is more than just a job. It is an opportunity to honour the trust that a customer has placed in us.

Our Journey Continues

What began in 2016 as a small shop called NATIONAL TRADERS, with the support of Mr. Abdullah, has grown into NATIONAL INTERIORS.

We are proud of how far we have come.

But we are even more excited about where we are going.

The struggles of our past keep us humble. The trust of our customers keeps us motivated. And the dream that began in 2016 continues to drive us forward.

NATIONAL INTERIORS Built from a Small Beginning. Strengthened by Struggle. Driven by Hard Work. Growing with Trust.
